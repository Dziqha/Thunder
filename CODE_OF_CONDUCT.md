# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Thunder a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful and constructive
- welcoming feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private report via repository contact channels or maintainer email.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.
